Privacy Policy

Plano Bath Remodeler LLC

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Plano Bath Remodeler collects when you contact us through this website or by phone/text, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address (if given), and any details about your project. If you call or text us, we retain the phone number and content of that call or message.

How we use your information

We use this information to respond to your request, schedule and confirm appointments, provide quotes, and follow up regarding your project. We may contact you by phone, text message, or email for these purposes.

SMS / text message data

We do not share SMS data outside our company or its affiliates, except where required by law or as necessary to provide the services you've requested. We do not sell SMS data, and no mobile information is shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information shared with contractors who perform support services (such as customer service or subcontracted installation work) is permitted where needed to complete your project.

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with us (e.g., appointment scheduling, follow-ups, service updates). Message and data rates may apply. All SMS communications are subject to your agreement with your mobile carrier at standard data and messaging rates.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP. For full details on text message consent, opting out, and getting help, see our SMS Terms & Conditions.

Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with contractors, installers, or service providers directly involved in completing the service you've requested, or where required by law.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Your choices

You can opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message. To ask us to update or remove information we hold about you, contact us using the information below.
